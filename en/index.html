Scientific writing · GNOME · LaTeX

Write in LaTeX.
Stay in your flow.

A native editor for your documents and references.

Ovenbird brings LaTeX document editing and a local bibliography into one app made for GNOME. Your files remain yours: simple, portable, and free from proprietary formats.

02 · Features

Everything you need to write, with your documents in place

01

Two modes, one document

Switch between Code and Visual while working on the same LaTeX content. Visual mode helps with common tasks while the original code stays accessible.

02

Local bibliography

Create, search, and organize references in a library independent from your projects. Add citations to LaTeX documents.

03

Import and export BibTeX

Import .bib files and export your library to share references across projects and tools.

04

Native on GNOME

Built with GTK 4 and Libadwaita, designed for Linux GNOME.

03 · How it works

Your files remain yours

Ovenbird works with familiar formats. Open a document or project folder and keep using your tools.

01

LaTeX, without conversion

Your documents are ordinary .tex files, with no closed format.

02

You choose the folder

Open files with the desktop chooser. The app does not ask for unrestricted access to your home folder.

03

Local first

Your library stays on your computer and works offline.
